VISUALIZATION GUIDE: Who-To-Follow and Node2Vec Rewiring Algorithms
======================================================================

This guide provides specific advice for illustrating WTF and Node2Vec algorithms
in the network rewiring quadrat figure (similar to local/bridge similar/opposite panels).

## WHO-TO-FOLLOW (WTF)

Mechanism: Build a "circle of trust" from focal node's neighbors, then recommend
who *they* follow (2-hop transitivity).

Visual recommendation:
- Focal node: Choose a well-connected node (e.g., a blue node on the left with 2-3 neighbors)
- Circle of trust: Add small highlight circles around the focal node's immediate neighbors
- Dotted line target: Draw to a node that is:
  * Connected to at least one member of the circle of trust
  * NOT currently connected to the focal node
  * At distance 2 from focal node (2-hop path)

Specific example using panel A's layout:
- Focal: Bottom-left blue node with multiple connections
- Highlight its 2-3 immediate neighbors (the trusted circle)
- Dotted line → a node (blue OR orange) that those neighbors connect to but focal doesn't

---

## NODE2VEC

Mechanism: Recommend nodes with similar structural embeddings (similar network
positions/roles), regardless of graph distance or attributes.

Visual recommendation:
- Focal node: Any node with distinct structural properties
- Dotted line target: A node with similar structural role:
  * Similar degree (both hubs or both peripheral)
  * Similar clustering coefficient
  * Similar betweenness centrality (both bridges or both embedded)
  * Can be far apart in the graph

Specific example:
- Connect two hub nodes (high degree) that are distant in the network
- OR connect two bridge nodes (high betweenness) from different communities
- The key: they share structural similarity, not proximity

---

## KEY VISUAL DISTINCTION

- WTF: Shows *local traversal* through the network (follow the path through trusted neighbors)
- Node2Vec: Shows *structural equivalence* that can jump across the network topology
  based on embedding similarity

---

Note: Both algorithms are CLASS-AGNOSTIC - they don't consider node attributes
(blue vs orange), only network structure.
